## Overview

The next submission period is from 1 February 2027 until 11 March 2027 4pm UK time.

Reporting project outcomes is a process during which funding recipients tell UKRI how their project is going. This happens using a platform called Researchfish.

If you are the lead researcher associated with a research grant or fellowship from UKRI, we ask you to provide an annual update on your project’s outcomes. It’s usually part of the terms and conditions of your award, unless you have an exemption.

You need to report your outcomes from early in your project until at least five years after it ends.

You can only submit your report once per year, during the submission period. However, you can update your outcomes all year round, and we encourage you to do so.

If you do not report, your funding may be withdrawn.

Research outcomes are made publicly available for the purposes of transparency and knowledge-sharing on UKRI’s [Gateway to Research](https://gtr.ukri.org/).
