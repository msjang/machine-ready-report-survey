# Gateway to Research

The Gateway to Research website has been developed by UK Research and Innovation (UKRI) to enable users to search for and analyse information about our funded research and innovation.

## Explore all projects, people, organisations, publications and outcomes

### <a href="/find-research-and-innovation/explore-what-weve-funded/projects" class="govuk-link">Browse all data</a>

View all UKRI funded research and innovation including projects, people, organisations, publications and outcomes. Refine the data using search and filters.

## Projects by status

### <a href="/find-research-and-innovation/explore-what-weve-funded/projects?status=Active" class="govuk-link">Browse 32,853 active projects</a>

View all research currently in progress. Filter the list by funding amount or region to find specific projects.

### <a href="/find-research-and-innovation/explore-what-weve-funded/projects?status=Closed" class="govuk-link">Browse 158,206 closed projects</a>

View all research previously completed. Filter the list by funding amount or region to find specific projects.

## Research and innovation outputs

### <a href="/find-research-and-innovation/explore-what-weve-funded/publications" class="govuk-link">Search over 1.2m publications</a>

Find external research and papers linked to projects. Narrow the results by type, funder or publication year to find specific references.

### <a href="/find-research-and-innovation/explore-what-weve-funded/outcomes" class="govuk-link">See over 1m project outcomes</a>

Explore the results of completed projects. You can filter by the type of output or funder.

## Projects by funded amount

### <a href="/find-research-and-innovation/explore-what-weve-funded/projects?amount=lt100k" class="govuk-link">Browse projects funded up to £100k</a>

More than 48k projects including small research and training grants, studentships, and fellowships.

### <a href="/find-research-and-innovation/explore-what-weve-funded/projects?amount=100k-1m" class="govuk-link">Browse projects funded from £100k to £1m</a>

More than 74k projects including medium size research and training grants, fellowships, and collaborative R&D.

### <a href="/find-research-and-innovation/explore-what-weve-funded/projects?amount=1m-10m" class="govuk-link">Browse projects funded from £1m to £10m</a>

More than 13k projects including large fellowships, research and training grants, and collaborative R&D.

### <a href="/find-research-and-innovation/explore-what-weve-funded/projects?amount=gte10m" class="govuk-link">Browse projects funded above £10m</a>

More than 754 projects including major research and training grants, BEIS funded programmes and centres.

## Project partners and contributors

### <a href="/find-research-and-innovation/explore-what-weve-funded/organisations" class="govuk-link">Browse over 103k participating organisations</a>

View the organisations involved in research projects. You can filter by region.

### <a href="/find-research-and-innovation/explore-what-weve-funded/people" class="govuk-link">Browse over 170k project contributors</a>

Find specialists and project contributors by searching GTR. Explore the complete list of individuals to identify expertise and research history.
